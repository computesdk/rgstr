New: `rgstr offer <name> [--amount <usd>] [--why <text>] [-y]` — an offer on an unclaimed name you cannot claim, plan → confirm → file like `claim` (the floor, the amount, the why and the rule: the maintainer can claim the name for free at any time during the 30-day window, and that beats your offer; the card is saved at the checkout URL the filing returns and charged only if the offer wins). `rgstr offers` lists your organization's offers; `rgstr offer --withdraw <name>` ends one. `registered` is a state everywhere the CLI prints one: `whois` (`registered by Acme Labs · identity verified · routes to @acme/fastcache · no maintainer has claimed this name`, plus any open offer), `search` (`registered (Acme Labs)`), `check` (`registered (Acme Labs → @acme/fastcache)`, summary adds `and K registered`). API errors `offer_below_floor`, `offer_identity_required`, `offer_limit_reached`, `name_claimed`, `route_target_not_claimed` print their hint last like every other.
